# Patrol: leaning books

The real robot uses its IMX500 as a camera and sends sampled frames to Roboflow Workflow `ok-ok-ziiym/Libot` with class `cardboard box`. This workflow class represents books in this patrol; detections are recorded as `LEANING_BOOK_PRESENT`. Install the robot requirements and configure `ROBOFLOW_API_KEY` in the environment of the camera service; never commit the key. Restart the camera service after changing it. The Pi must have network access to `https://serverless.roboflow.com` and the Roboflow account must have access to the workflow.

Segmentation masks with confidence at least 0.70 are measured against vertical in the image. A book angle of at least 15 degrees in three consecutive inference results creates an event with the original JPEG, confidence, angle and waypoint. Very small and nearly square masks are ignored. The camera must be level: perspective, occlusion, shape and mounting angle can affect the measurement. This is an image-plane estimate, not a physical inclination measurement. The service performs at most one inference at a time, sampling no faster than once per second. Cloud calls may incur Roboflow charges.

The workflow sends evidence to the backend and includes it in the patrol report. Detector errors fail the patrol move and are surfaced in its result. If a cloud request times out or credentials are invalid, check network access and the service configuration, then restart the camera service before retrying. No local patrol model or label file is required; barcode and QR scanning still use the camera service.
